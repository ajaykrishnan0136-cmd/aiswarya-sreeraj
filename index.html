Dakshina

Simple · elegant · traditional

Please wear
Cream
Kasavu gold

Plain Kerala kasavu only, with no prints or colours. Kasavu saree, skirt and blouse or a light kurta for women, and kurta or shirt with mundu for men. Minimal jewellery.

Davani / half saree is reserved for the bride.
